Financial Intelligence

Finance Teams Spend Too Much Time on Data, Not Enough on Decisions

The average finance team spends 60% of its time collecting and cleaning data. What's left for the analysis that actually drives decisions? Not enough.

The CFO Agent connects to your ERP, accounting system, and reporting tools to automate the grunt work — variance pulls, close checklists, board prep, and cash flow forecasting — so your team can do the work that actually matters.

What It Does

A Dedicated AI Agent for Finance Leaders

Custom-connected to your systems. Trained on your reporting cadence. Running from day one.

Month-End Close Automation

Runs your close checklist, flags open items, reconciles accounts, and surfaces variances before the deadline — not after.

Variance Analysis

Pulls actuals versus budget, flags anomalies, and drafts the narrative your board expects. Ready before your first review meeting.

Cash Flow Forecasting

Models 13-week and rolling cash positions using your live AR, AP, and payroll data. Flags coverage gaps before they become problems.

Board and Investor Prep

Compiles the financial package, drafts commentary, and formats the deck. You review and present. Everything else is handled.

Accounts Receivable Monitoring

Flags aging invoices, drafts collection outreach, and keeps your DSO from creeping without you having to chase it.

Audit and Compliance Readiness

Maintains documentation, tracks policy adherence, and flags gaps before your auditors do. No more last-minute fire drills.
